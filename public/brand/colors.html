Colors

Two brand colors do most of the work: Violet for accents and interactive surfaces, and Ink for type and the default filled button. Everything else is restraint — a single neutral ramp and four status hues lifted from the live PrimeNG theme.

1.1 Brand

Violet is the only accent you should reach for by default. Pair it with Ink for contrast; never pair it with another saturated hue in the same component.

⚑
Rule of one accent. Use Violet or Yellow in a single surface — never both at full saturation. Yellow is reserved for BonusPrize and highlight tokens; Pink/Cyan appear only on landing illustrations.

1.2 Interactive states

Violet has three explicit states. Ink gets soft hover/active that read as a 10% lift — do not muddy it with a second hue.

1.4 Status

One pair per state — a solid fill and a 5%-tint background. Do not invent new status colors; map to these.

1.5 Contrast & usage guardrails

WCAG-AA body text requires 4.5:1. Our Ink-on-White is 18:1 and Violet-on-White is 5.8:1 (AA for body, AAA for large). Violet on Ink fails AA — never place body copy in that pairing.
