Buttons & Badges

Filled Ink is the workhorse — 90% of primary actions. Violet "secondary" is for the one call-to-action on a page that has to sing (Upgrade, Canjear, Crear campaña). Outlined and Text are for tertiary moves. Status colors are for destructive and affirming confirmations only.
